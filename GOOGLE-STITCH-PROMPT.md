# Google Stitch prompt — Yugrow

Use this as the prompt for a new Google Stitch project. Attach the Yugrow agency plan, the supplied liquid-chrome portrait, and the local Flow Lines and Node Garden SVGs as references.

---

Design a complete responsive landing page for **YUGROW**, a growth team helping D2C brands grow revenue and creators grow audiences. The page should feel like an original digital growth studio with the color, scale, and confidence of maximalist/editorial brutalism. It must still read clearly as a useful growth-team website.

## Core idea

**Growth starts with people.** Make the supplied liquid-chrome face the central human metaphor: a continuously moving, dimensional portrait represents the people at the other end of a growth system. Put the portrait into the opening scene at large scale. Connect it to three labeled stages—Attention, Trust, Action—so the lines have a clear purpose. The face, its motion, the typography, and the customer journey should belong to one system.

## Visual language

Build a deliberate palette from acid lime, electric cobalt, warm paper, signal red, saturated pink, mint, and near-black. Use bold condensed display type, oversized editorial headlines, tightly controlled serif italic accents, hard borders, ruled labels, and abrupt color changes between sections. Compose with strong asymmetry and generous readable copy. Make the page rich in color without adding gradients as a shortcut.

Design a separate **single-color identity** for Yugrow. The symbol is an open, continuous loop that curls inward and exits through a gap: it suggests a growth system that learns and starts another cycle. Keep it abstract and recognisable at favicon size; use one flat ink color and no gradients, multicolor nodes, leaves, arrows, or stock upward bars. Pair it with a custom-feeling but legible YUGROW wordmark. This logo remains monochrome even though the website itself is colorful.

Create scene-to-scene pacing: a high-energy hero, a warm-paper story beat, a mint journey map, a dark network section, a sharp lime process section, a pink-and-blue creative lab, then a high-contrast brief builder. Motion should explain the story—show a signal branching into channels, connect creators and audiences, animate the portrait's reflective surface, and make the creative specimen change as the user selects a job.

## Page structure

1. **Hero:** Yugrow mark; headline “Make growth mean more.” Copy: “We connect strategy, creative, distribution and customer experience so brands grow revenue and creators grow audiences.” Add “Find your growth path” and “See the method” actions. Feature the animated chrome face with the purposeful Attention, Trust, Action path.
2. **The idea:** Explain how a story travels from a message to a channel to a response. Connect the steps: Find the signal → Shape the story → Carry it forward → Learn and improve.
3. **Two growth paths:** Accessible tabs for D2C Brand and Creator. Each route changes its headline, map labels, explanatory copy, and service list. D2C work can include social/content, creative, Meta/Google ads, landing pages/CRO, influencer, retention, and analytics. Creator work can include audience strategy, scripting/editing, packaging, repurposing/distribution, brand partnerships, and monetization.
4. **People and channels:** Use Node Garden as an audience/creator/community/channel network map. Explain why relevance and a useful connection matter more than reach alone.
5. **Delivery system:** Discovery → Audit → Strategy → Create → Distribute → Optimize → Report. Each stage must be selectable and explain what it does. Show that reporting informs the next discovery.
6. **Creative that travels:** Make a visually rich motion specimen with selectable “Stop / Show / Invite” jobs. Each selection changes the visual and text to explain how a hook earns a pause, a reveal makes the value clear, and an invitation gives a next step. Label it a demonstration, not client work.
7. **Brief builder:** A concise way to select D2C/Creator and the current growth challenge. The primary action may copy a starting brief locally; do not imply it is sent to Yugrow.

## Inspiration to translate

- **Naked City Films:** Use movement and scene transitions as the storytelling structure.
- **NEW SUSHISM:** Let a human-centered visual and oversized type establish the story immediately.
- **Ponpon Mania:** Bring in saturated color, a distinctive visual world, and a chapter-like rhythm.
- Earlier references include FlowFest, Eloy Benoffi, Sui Overflow, Slosh Seltzer, and The Geek Designer. Translate their pacing, contrast, and graphic confidence; do not reuse their text or assets.

The supplied Flow Lines SVG should explain how a message moves through channels. Node Garden should explain audience and creator relationships. Use either only where it serves that purpose.

## Interaction and accessibility

Use real dimensional motion for the chrome portrait if the renderer supports it, with a still fallback if it does not. Let pointer movement affect the scene subtly. Keep scroll under user control. Include accessible, keyboard-operable tabs, mobile navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, and responsive layouts. All information and controls should remain readable without WebGL.

## Guardrails

Do not include founders. Do not invent clients, testimonials, results, metrics, awards, prices, credentials, contact information, or a submission endpoint. Keep every visual element connected to people, audience, message, creative, distribution, conversion, retention, or learning. Do not make a generic SaaS dashboard, an arbitrary stack of cards, or a page filled with decorative floating shapes. Avoid purple gradients and meaningless motion.
