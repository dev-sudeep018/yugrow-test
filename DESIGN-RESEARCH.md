# Yugrow design research

References reviewed for this redesign. Notes describe specific ideas observed on each site; the Yugrow page uses its own copy and locally stored assets.

## Three additional maximalist / brutalist references

### Naked City Films — https://www.nakedcityfilms.com/

The opening screen uses a strong, contained identity over full-bleed film imagery. A repeating scroll cue and scene-like pacing make motion part of the navigation. **Applied to Yugrow:** clear chapter labels and transitions between purposeful color scenes, with the chrome portrait as the opening visual focus.

### NEW SUSHISM — https://new-sushism.jp/

Its first impression is human and photographic: a close-up artisan image carries the story while large lettering and chapter navigation make the subject unmistakable. **Applied to Yugrow:** a human portrait leads the hero; the growth story is organized as a sequence with typography that shares the composition.

### Ponpon Mania — https://ponpon-mania.com/

The page builds a distinctive illustrated world through saturated color, oversized lettering, and a chapter-based interactive comic experience. **Applied to Yugrow:** high-contrast color scenes and a clear sequence through the page, with the interactive graphics tied to real parts of the growth offer.

## Shared design decision

The references point to a page with a point of view, but Yugrow's service and audience need to stay legible. The build uses big condensed type, graphic rules, color changes, and motion; diagrams explain the customer journey, creator/channel relationships, and delivery stages. Flow Lines and Node Garden from Book of Shapes are placed in the story and network sections for those jobs. The face supplied by the user is animated locally as a displaced Three.js surface.

## Yugrow mark

The previous four-color crossing-and-node icon was replaced with a single-stroke open loop. Its path turns back through itself and leaves a visible opening: growth is a cycle that learns, while the opening keeps it from reading as a closed system. The mark uses one ink color on light surfaces and a one-color reversed treatment on dark surfaces. Its shape is not made from an arrow, sprout, or generic node network. Anime.js draws the path in once on entry.

## Product and content guardrails

The agency plan supplies the D2C and creator service categories and a seven-step process. The page uses those as its content structure. It omits founder references, client stories, testimonials, and performance numbers that the plan does not substantiate.

## Starred design repositories reviewed

The user's starred GitHub design repositories were ranked by observed star counts during review. The most popular three design resources examined were:

- [ui-ux-pro-max-skill](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill) — design intelligence and interface guidance.
- [awesome-design-md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md) — a library of design-system references.
- [taste-skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill) — guidance focused on stronger, less generic visual direction.

Their downloaded guidance informed hierarchy, contrast, component behavior, and visual specificity. Their files and design systems were not copied wholesale.
